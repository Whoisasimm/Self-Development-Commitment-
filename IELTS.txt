==================================================
DAY 05 — IELTS FOUNDATION
READING | WRITING | LISTENING | SPEAKING
==================================================

GOAL:
Build the complete IELTS foundation before starting
advanced Band 7+ strategies.

IELTS HAS 4 SECTIONS:
1. Listening
2. Reading
3. Writing
4. Speaking


==================================================
1. IELTS TEST TYPES
==================================================

IELTS Academic:
Usually used for university / higher education /
professional registration.

IELTS General Training:
Usually used for work, migration and general purposes.

IMPORTANT:
Listening and Speaking are essentially the same
structure in Academic and General Training.

Reading and Writing are different between Academic
and General Training.


==================================================
2. LISTENING — BASICS
==================================================

FORMAT:
- 4 parts
- 40 questions
- Audio is played once
- Different accents can appear
- You must follow the word limit exactly

COMMON QUESTION TYPES:
- Form completion
- Note completion
- Table completion
- Summary completion
- Sentence completion
- Multiple choice
- Matching
- Map / plan labeling
- Diagram labeling
- Short answer

MAIN SKILLS:

1. Predict the answer
Before the audio starts, look at the question
and predict what type of answer is required.

Example:
"She will arrive at ______."

Likely answer:
A time / date.

2. Listen for paraphrases

Question:
"The course starts in September."

Audio:
"The program begins in the ninth month of the year."

Same meaning, different words.

3. Watch spelling carefully.

4. Watch singular/plural forms.

5. Follow the word limit.

If the instruction says:
"NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS"

Do not write three words.

6. Do not panic after missing one answer.
Move immediately to the next question.


LISTENING MISTAKE:
Trying to understand every single word.

BETTER:
Understand the meaning and find the required answer.


==================================================
3. READING — BASICS
==================================================

ACADEMIC READING:
- 3 sections
- 40 questions
- 60 minutes

GENERAL TRAINING READING:
- 3 sections
- 40 questions
- 60 minutes

COMMON QUESTION TYPES:

1. Multiple Choice

2. TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN

TRUE:
The statement agrees with the passage.

FALSE:
The statement contradicts the passage.

NOT GIVEN:
The passage does not provide enough information.

IMPORTANT:
NOT GIVEN does NOT mean FALSE.

3. YES / NO / NOT GIVEN

Used mainly for writer's views or claims.

YES:
Writer agrees.

NO:
Writer disagrees.

NOT GIVEN:
Writer's opinion is not provided.

4. Matching Headings

Choose the heading that best represents
the main idea of a paragraph.

5. Matching Information

Find which paragraph contains specific information.

6. Sentence Completion

Complete the sentence using information from
the passage.

7. Summary / Note / Table / Flow-chart Completion

Find the relevant information and complete the blanks.

8. Diagram Labeling

Identify information from the passage.

9. Short Answer Questions


READING CORE SKILLS:

SKIMMING:
Read quickly to understand the general idea.

SCANNING:
Search quickly for a specific word, name, number,
date or idea.

PARAPHRASING:
IELTS often changes the words used in the passage.

Example:

Question:
"rapid increase"

Passage:
"grew dramatically"

Same basic meaning.

KEYWORD TRAP:
Do not automatically choose an answer just because
you see the exact same word.

Always check the meaning.


READING RULE:
The answer must be supported by the passage.

Do NOT use your personal knowledge.


==================================================
4. WRITING — BASICS
==================================================

IELTS WRITING = 2 TASKS

TASK 1:
Academic:
Graph / chart / table / process / map

General Training:
Letter

Minimum:
150 words

Recommended time:
About 20 minutes


TASK 2:
Essay

Minimum:
250 words

Recommended time:
About 40 minutes

TASK 2 HAS MORE WEIGHT THAN TASK 1.


ACADEMIC TASK 1 STRUCTURE:

1. Introduction
Paraphrase the question.

2. Overview
Give the main overall trends / important features.

3. Body Paragraph 1
Important details.

4. Body Paragraph 2
Important details / comparisons.


IMPORTANT:
Do NOT simply describe every number.

Focus on:
- Main trends
- Highest / lowest
- Major changes
- Important comparisons


TASK 2 COMMON ESSAY TYPES:

1. Opinion
"Do you agree or disagree?"

2. Discussion
"Discuss both views and give your opinion."

3. Advantages / Disadvantages

4. Problem / Solution

5. Two-part question


BASIC TASK 2 STRUCTURE:

Introduction
↓
Body Paragraph 1
↓
Body Paragraph 2
↓
Conclusion


BODY PARAGRAPH BASIC LOGIC:

POINT
↓
EXPLANATION
↓
EXAMPLE
↓
RESULT / LINK


Example logic:

Point:
Online education is convenient.

Explanation:
Students can study from different locations.

Example:
A student living far from a university can attend
classes remotely.

Result:
This can increase access to education.


WRITING SCORING CRITERIA:

1. Task Achievement / Task Response
2. Coherence and Cohesion
3. Lexical Resource
4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy


IMPORTANT:
Good IELTS writing is NOT about using extremely
complicated vocabulary everywhere.

It is about:
- Clear ideas
- Relevant ideas
- Logical organization
- Accurate grammar
- Appropriate vocabulary
- Good paragraphing

5. SPEAKING — BASICS
==================================================

Speaking duration:
Approximately 11–14 minutes.

3 PARTS:

PART 1:
Short questions about familiar topics.

Examples:
- Home
- Studies
- Work
- Hobbies
- Food
- Technology
- Family
- Daily life

Goal:
Answer naturally and extend your answer.


PART 2:
Long turn.

You receive a topic/card.

You get:
- 1 minute preparation
- About 1–2 minutes speaking

Basic structure:

WHAT
↓
WHEN / WHERE
↓
DETAILS
↓
WHY
↓
FEELING / RESULT


PART 3:
More abstract discussion.

Questions become deeper.

Part 1:
"Do you like technology?"

Part 3:
"How has technology changed society?"


SPEAKING SCORING CRITERIA:

1. Fluency and Coherence
2. Lexical Resource
3. Grammatical Range and Accuracy
4. Pronunciation


SPEAKING RULE:

Do NOT memorize huge answers.

Instead:

ANSWER
+
REASON
+
EXAMPLE
+
RESULT / PERSONAL EXPERIENCE


Example:

Question:
"Do you like reading?"

Weak:
"Yes, I like reading."

Better:
"Yes, I enjoy reading because it helps me learn
new ideas. For example, I often read technical
articles about artificial intelligence, especially
when I am learning a new topic."


==================================================
6. THE 4 IELTS SKILLS
==================================================

LISTENING:
Hear → Understand → Locate → Answer

READING:
Skim → Locate → Understand → Answer

WRITING:
Understand → Plan → Organize → Write → Check

SPEAKING:
Understand → Answer → Explain → Example → Extend


==================================================
7. COMMON IELTS MISTAKES
==================================================

LISTENING:
- Losing focus after one missed answer
- Spelling mistakes
- Ignoring word limits
- Not predicting answers

READING:
- Reading every word slowly
- Using outside knowledge
- Confusing FALSE and NOT GIVEN
- Spending too much time on one question

WRITING:
- No clear position
- Weak paragraph structure
- No overview in Academic Task 1
- Repeating the same vocabulary
- Memorized essays
- Poor grammar
- Writing irrelevant information

SPEAKING:
- One-line answers
- Memorized answers
- Speaking too fast
- Ignoring the question
- Fear of making small mistakes
- Giving no examples or explanations
